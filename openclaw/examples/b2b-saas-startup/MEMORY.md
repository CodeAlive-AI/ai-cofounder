# BillSync — CEO OS Memory

## Company

**BillSync** — AI billing assistant for professional services firms. Auto-generates invoices from time entries, reconciles billable hours, and surfaces margin leaks in real time.

**Web:** https://billsync.example | **Founder:** Alex Chen (alex@billsync.example)

**Stage:** Pre-revenue, pre-PMF
**Team:** 4 (founder + 2 engineers + 1 designer). Strong in product, weak in sales/marketing. Looking for first bizdev hire.

## Current Wedge

**ICP:** COO / Head of Operations at consulting firms with 50-500 billable consultants, manual or semi-automated time tracking, multiple billing rates per project
**Pain:** Monthly invoicing takes 3-5 days of partner time. Reconciliation errors cost 2-4% of revenue. Margins on T&M projects are invisible until quarter end.
**Promise:** "Close the books in hours, not days — without changing your time-tracking tool."
**Competitors:** Harvest, Toggl, BigTime, FreshBooks

## PMF Stage: 0-1 (Search / early wedge proof)

Gate 1 (Pain proof): in progress | Gate 2 (Value proof): partial | Gate 3 (Repeatability): not started | Gate 4 (Unit economics): not started

## Products

- Auto-Invoicer (LLM-assisted invoice draft from raw time entries + project rules)
- Time Reconciler (catches missing/double-counted hours before they hit the invoice)
- Margin Analyzer (live margin per project + early-warning when a fixed-fee project starts losing money)

## System Constraint (Critical Chain)

**Constraint:** Rate of validated market learning — speed at which founder converts market contact into validated beliefs about the wedge.

**Why this is the constraint:** Team is product-strong, build velocity is fine, but "sales are slow", 3 signups in 7 days, LinkedIn outreach = 0 conversions. Bottleneck is not product, it's market contact.

**5 Focusing Steps (Goldratt):**
1. Identify: founder-market contact -> validated beliefs
2. Exploit: maximize CEO hours on customer conversations, discovery, sales
3. Subordinate: everything else (product, infra, content) serves the constraint
4. Elevate: when exploit exhausted — hire bizdev/sales
5. Repeat: when constraint shifts — reassess
